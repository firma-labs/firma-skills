# Restraint over flash

"if done wrong just having shiny tilt cards doesn't show anything impressive and can look bad."

Effects (tilt, curtain, glow) only where they earn it, and subtle. Lead with fundamentals. Match the calm of the existing surface and make it more refined, never busier.

**"Premium" means elevate, not replace.** On the Codex cards pass, wholesale swaps made a liked dashboard worse and stripped the real Slack, Gmail, Notion, Linear, and ClickUp logos: "you removed the real logos and that's the best part." The one surface that landed was a specific, requested transformation.

**How:** base any polish on the real live version, not a rebuild from a screenshot. Only add; never remove real assets, logos, imagery, or working layout. For "premium", ask for a reference whose feel he likes and calibrate to it. Do not touch surfaces he says he likes. See [[image-rules]].
