# super-firma (blue)

**Colors.** Page `#f5f9ff`, text `#0b1015`, blue `#0562ef` (accent `#1e78ff`, hover `#3782f2`), border `#c5dbf2`, dark ground `#0b1015`, green `#06D6A0` for success only.

**Fonts.** Space Grotesk for headings, logos, and stat values (ligatures off on "firma"); DM Sans for body; DM Mono for labels and tags.

**Patterns.** Centered 1200px nav with frosted glass. Sticky section labels in system monospace with an `[N/8]` counter. Borders not shadows. No radius on structural elements.

**Page rhythm:** light sections, at most three dark spotlights, exactly one blue band placed between content and the dark close, no back-to-back dark sections. See [[page-rhythm]].

**Diagram rules:** light ground only, hexagons for nodes, leader-line callouts, at most two colors, no filters or glow or animation, bezier connections, isometric for layered views.

The acid variant in [[super-firma-acid-dither]] is the default now.
