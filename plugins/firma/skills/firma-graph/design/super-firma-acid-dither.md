# super-firma-acid-dither

Use this first for any document or artifact unless Curtis says otherwise. Canonical example: the Code of Conduct artifact (`the-more-excellent-way-final.html`), self-contained with embedded dithers.

**Palette.** Canvas `#ffffff`, wells `#faf9f6`. Ink is warm charcoal `#38353b`, never pure black. Muted `rgba(56,53,59,.64)`. Borders `#e7e3e6`. Accent acid yellow `#f7ff05` (press `#e6ee00`, readable line `#cdd400`, ink-on-accent `#2b2930`). Warm dark ground `#2b2930`.

**Fonts.** Space Grotesk for display and wordmark (ligatures off on "firma"), DM Sans for body, DM Mono for labels and tags.

**Accent rules.** Yellow is a fill, highlight, underline, tick, or band. Never body or label text on white. Key words get a yellow highlight behind them, not colored text. A standout band is solid yellow with charcoal text. On dark grounds, yellow may be used for labels and numbers.

**Section headers.** The section NAME is the big title, the number sits in an acid chip, and the descriptive line goes below as a subordinate dek. Never make the kicker the name and a sentence the title.

**Lists must scan:** a big ghosted number, a bold headline line, a small pill tag, then body. Not tiny numbers and repeated thin rules.

**Dithers** come from the dither folder at full strength as bordered image cells or split bands beside copy; see [[image-rules]]. Components: topbar and meta strip, bordered section frames (borders not shadows), pull quote with accent bar, dark spotlight, dither media cell, split band, acid band, two-column article grid, close. See [[super-firma-blue]] for the original and [[page-rhythm]] for the rhythm.
