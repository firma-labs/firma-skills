# Mobile first

Curtis, "really disappointed": "Nobody's gonna do that stuff on desktop. It's only gonna be on mobile."

Every Colony app ships one shared mobile structure: the masthead, a bottom bar and hex-ring menu (a designed menu, not an average hamburger), bottom-safe scrolling, modern mobile principles. Increase (the habit game) may feel a little different but must feel like family.

A page that cannot scroll to its bottom on a phone is a defect. Verify at 390px in the browser before showing him. When the team is about to see it, ship the standard fast, breadth over polish, then refine. See [[colony-design-law]] and [[hive-masthead]].
