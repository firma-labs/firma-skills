# Firma Edge design system

**Tokens.** Edge black `#171716` (never `#000`) · panel dark `#242422` · raised `#302f2b` · infrastructure white `#f1efe8` (warm bone) · panel `#fafaf6` · pure `#ffffff` for pop · Field Orange `#ff5a1f` (THE accent; hover `#e6531c`) · realm mint `#c8dccb` · sage `#5e8a68` · information blue `#afcfea` · concrete tan `#d7cfb5` · structural olive `#7a6b43` · warning amber `#e2a93b` · critical `#c9483d` · offline `#6c6c67`. No navy, no purple, no gradients, no glow.

**Type.** Geist for text; Geist Mono for labels in small caps with wide tracking. Self-hosted woff2 in the pack.

**Shape.** Radii 0 to 6px only. No pills, no glass, no shadows. Square status indicators. Blueprint grounds: tan, sage, or dark with a 24px drafting grid and 96px plus-marks. Corner-bracket frames. Document plates (`FE-<PRODUCT>-<TYPE>-<NNN> · REV X`). Rail labels, hazard stripes, spec tables with VERIFIED / PROPOSED / TARGET marks, a maturity ladder.

**Three modes:** Product · Operations (dark) · Technical (light).

Orange means live, hot, or selected. Motion only where it means something: lines run, a touched system lights, a panel slides. Nothing bounces. The [[blueprint]] is drawn in this system. Renders live in [[asset-library]]. Colony apps borrow the palette through [[colony-design-law]].
