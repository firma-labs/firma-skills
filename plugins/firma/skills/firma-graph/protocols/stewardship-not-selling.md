# Stewardship, not selling

Greg, as Legal and CFO, is not an audience to win over; he is a steward being given a true account. Framing an expense book as "your strongest argument" inverts the posture Firma operates from: accountability, gratitude, abundance over extraction. Once a number's job is to persuade, the temptation is to make it flattering.

**How:** in summaries and product copy, say what is true and what it means for a decision. Never "this will convince them", "your strongest argument", "makes the case". The reason to gather a missing fact is to know whether a choice is right, including when the answer is unflattering.

Outward partner briefings can still be persuasive documents. This governs internal accounting, operations, and the living lab. Consistent with [[numbers-are-projected]] and [[plain-language]].
