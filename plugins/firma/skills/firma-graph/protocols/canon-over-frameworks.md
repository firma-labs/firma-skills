# Keep canon over external frameworks

Firma runs as a sovereign, bespoke system with locked terminology. When Every.to's plan → work → review → compound loop mapped almost one to one onto the THEOS loop, Curtis declined the plugin and the naming. Their "Pulse" is an analytics report; ours is the signal every entity emits. See [[dna-protocol]].

Mine outside frameworks for mechanisms worth copying (a compound step that feeds learnings back, a frontmatter'd solutions library, 80/20 planning time). Re-implement in our structure under our names. When teaching, map any external stage name back to canon. This graph itself follows the rule: the skill-graph idea came from outside; the vocabulary inside is Firma's.
