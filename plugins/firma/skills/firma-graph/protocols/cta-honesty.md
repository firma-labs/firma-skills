# CTA honesty

**Never on a public surface:** "Schedule Deployment", "Schedule a Deployment Review", "Spec a [Product]", "Get Started" (when it implies an install), "Configure Yours", "Start Trial" (when no trial exists).

**Use:** "Join the Deployment Waitlist", "Join Us", "Sign Up", "Apply", "Get in touch", "Open roles".

Honest framing: "We're building, and we're starting to onboard pilot communities. Sign up and we'll talk when we're ready to deploy with you."

**Doc-internal surfaces** (anything behind the portal login) need no transactional CTA at all. Body links are navigational: sibling pages, back to the index. The standard back button is `← Firma Labs`. Nav CTA on the dark product pages: `Login` and `Start building →`.

This is the same discipline as [[numbers-are-projected]] applied to buttons.
