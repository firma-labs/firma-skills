# Team docs, plain

Curtis killed "the battery is an hour, the towel is the day." No cryptic aphorisms, no unexplained house metaphors in team-facing docs. "The three personality assessments", not "the battery". "The four core documents", not "the spine". No "promote past the towel", no "Ratchet" in onboarding docs. "Weighted most heavily", not "super-weighted".

Google Docs are clean native Docs: regular headings, bold, tables, bullets. No dither headers, no custom fonts, no styled-HTML mimicry ("the google docs are awful looking… make them regular"). See [[plain-language]] and [[formation]].
