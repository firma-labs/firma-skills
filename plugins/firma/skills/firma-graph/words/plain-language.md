# Plain language

Curtis on a screen full of "membrane posture" and "projected FIG reserve signal": "i have no idea what it all means - and i'm the person who thought of it."

**Keep** the locked nouns: Settlemint, Realm, Realm Node, SeedBase, Haven, Photon, Ion+, firmamint, elemint. See [[names-and-casing]].

**Replace** everything else with what it means to a person. "Power it makes", not "projected generation". "Homes", not "projected housing units". "Care and schooling", not "civic support". "Ready to stand on its own", not "sovereignty readiness". Say a tradeoff as a consequence ("you will run short until you add Photon"), not as a metric.

**In team docs**, no insider shorthand or unexplained house metaphors: "the three assessments", not "the battery"; "the four core documents", not "the spine"; no "promote past the towel", no "Ratchet" in onboarding docs. A first-day reader must understand every line. See [[team-docs-plain]].

A spec is written for builders; a screen is read by strangers. If a line needs a sentence, write the sentence.
