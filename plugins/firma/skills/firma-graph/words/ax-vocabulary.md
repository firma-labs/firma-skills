# AX vocabulary

Firma's internal vocabulary (AX, Materializing, MAX, Saint for the person, surface for the screen) is preferred in product framing and Saint-facing copy. Standard engineering words (UI, UX, screen, view, render, user) stay in specs and docs where they aid clarity, with an asterisk or a vocabulary note pointing to the Firma word.

The point is the architectural distinction (both Saint and agent are subjects; surfaces compose at runtime), not a vocabulary crusade. Never do a global search-and-replace of UI → surface or user → Saint. See [[theos]].
