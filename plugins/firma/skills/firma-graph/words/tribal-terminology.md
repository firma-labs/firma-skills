# Tribal terminology

Never write "Indian Country" in Firma or Nation of Heaven content. It is an older US legal phrasing that positions tribes as a federal jurisdiction. Firma's framing positions tribes as sovereign nations standing beside the Nation of Heaven as peers.

- **Tribal Network State(s)** in headlines, section titles, and structural references.
- **tribal land** in body copy.

Ground any approach to a tribe in the tribe's own assets and corridor logic, never in a teammate's personal connections. Greg Lovett has no tribal connection; see [[roles]]. Do not offer the future alliance as a present benefit; see [[fans-is-future]].
