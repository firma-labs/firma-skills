# Names and casing

**Always lowercase:** firmamint · atonemint · elemint · basemint · figmint · governmint · deploymints · theo (the agent, even at sentence start) · the fund names "terra firma fund" and "firma foundation grants".

**All caps:** FIRMA as the parent mark ("FIRMA Labs", "FIRMA Foundation") · THEOS · FIG · CIK · DNA.

**Title case, exactly as written:** Embodimint · Fragmint · Realm · Realm Node · Haven · SeedBase · Settlemint · Photon · Ion+ · Salt Ion+ · Atmos · Edge Cache · Sentinel · Mind · ARK · Firmagentics · DNA Protocol · Nation of Heaven · 1Accord Commons (capital C; never "the commons" when you mean this one) · Theosis (the theological term stays Title Case).

**Spellings that matter:** Convertor (never Converter) · Kouplen (not Kouplan) · Buda, Texas (not Budu) · "Fragmint slice" (never shard) · "vault claim" (PROPOSED word).

**Notation:** elemints are written e(photon), e(compute), e(H2O), e(Au), e(terra), e(BTC). The parentheses are part of the name.

**Roles:** Curtis is Vision. Never Founder, never CEO. Foundation Resolutions, never "General Partner decisions".

**Open:** the May typography rule made settlemint and seedbase lowercase; the September Blueprint and Curtis's own writing use Settlemint and SeedBase. Follow the Blueprint on screens until ruled. See [[killed-words]], and the "compute substrate" framing in [[firmamint]].
