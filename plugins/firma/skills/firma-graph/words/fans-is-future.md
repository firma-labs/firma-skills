# FANS is future, not present

The Firma Alliance of Network States (FANS) is a future architectural concept. Presenting a "FANS table seat" or "FANS political weight" to a first signer is vaporware and undermines the real partnership on offer.

**What is present and operative:** a Mutual Sovereignty Pact (peer standing between sovereigns now), protection that moves both ways, honor as the operating principle. The trio **sovereignty · honor · protection** is the language.

FANS may appear in internal architecture and governance docs, where its status is understood (it is the middle ring beside the citizen ring in [[embodimint]]). Never to an outside partner as present tense.
