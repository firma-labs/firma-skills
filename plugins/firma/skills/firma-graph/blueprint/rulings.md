# Rulings that shape the drawing


- Domains (Foundation · Capital · Labs · Theosis · Frontier) are a **filter** over the machine, not a tree. Research sits inside Labs. Edge and Photon are Spheres inside Labs.
- People live at the **center** as Embodimint, touching every layer. Not on the outer ring.
- Three concentric rings is the recurring grammar at every scale: citizen of the Nation of Heaven / the alliance beside it (FANS) / outside the Canon. Also: Foundation public hat / quiet vehicles / sealed core. Also: fingerprint / vault claim / market.
- Place ladder: Person → Household → Realm → Settlemint → District → Network State, with Constellation beside it. Realm is not a location on the ladder: it is the personal edge a person or household holds and carries between places, never owned by the Settlemint. The Nation of Heaven is a Network State: the one Firma is building. Composition, not containment. No "Colony" rung.
- One Settlemint ladder T0–T4. T3 Sovereign = a community's own sovereign treasury. The word SWF is reserved for the Nation's sealed fund and never appears as a rung.
- FIG floats. Settlemint local coins (figmints) are pegged to FIG. An elemint is one non-transferable fingerprint, e(photon) e(H2O) e(Au) e(BTC) style, signed to its host's Embodimint, never a token. A vault claim is the tradeable piece, issued only against a vaulted elemint, one claim per elemint.
- Fragmints are on-chain representation of you and your slice of the pie: inheritable, never transferable, connected to Embodimint. Fragmint capacity ("reap what you sow") is the founding principle from 2021, never presented as new.
- Atonemint makes the system whole after every trade. Reconciliation is done by the system, never forced on people. Convertors (Photon, Compute, Storage, Bandwidth, Water proposed) are agentic layers of Atonemint running Commons Netting. Photon Convertor's home is hardware, with a line to Atonemint.
- Elemint is drawn in its 2023 shape: treasury and custody, the RWA chain with the custody state machine (in-ground → raw → transit → refined → vaulted), RWA banking, trading, exploration and mining, the RWA fund.
- Governed spine, vertical: Canon → DNA → THEOS → Firmagentics → theo. Five machines inside the THEOS box.
- Edge Cache is THE capital block: one Edge Compute product that earns whether it does storage, compute, inference, or mining ("cache and cash"). Outputs e(compute) and e(BTC). Its waste heat warms the dwelling. Lives in every SeedBase. The word "Mint" is retired for the block.
- Haven = a dwelling (home, office, or community space), sizes One · Two · Three · custom. SeedBase = a community Haven with every Edge and Realm system for self-sufficiency, Edge Cache included. Both are modular kits packaged with purpose, added like blocks as a place forms. Old sub-terms (Hub, cafe, pods, Lux) are gone.
- Water Realm is not a separate product: a Realm node plus a water capture kit (tank, meter, first-flush, cooling loop). Realm is never divided by element. Star Realm is connectivity like Starlink, under Realm, not orbital compute.
- Orbital compute is one PROPOSED part, "Orbital Edge". All prior product names for it are pulled.
- Banks: Roca Bank is the Puerto Rico charter (international license, Atonemint Sovereign Gateway). Regent Bank is direct. Old Glory Bank is out.
- Funds: Firma Terra, Onym, Firma Ventures under Capital. Firma Frontier Fund under the Frontier Domain. Treasury is not a sovereign fund: Ecosystem Treasury (Capital) plus community treasuries (Allotmint, Mintware). Allotmint and the Water Realm ledger are Settlemint's financial layer, not Frontier's.
- Theosis: Formation Academy forms CIK, the Dreaded Ones, Transfiguration Lab, Ancient Future, and Tekton. CIK is ministry deployment only.
- Curtis is **Vision**. Greg is Legal and CFO. All numbers are PROPOSED until a real deployment validates them.

## 2026-09-13 — the document library

- The Portal's Documents area is filed by **what a document is for**, not by entity or project. Ten headings: Briefings · The Canon · Theosis · firmamint · THEOS · Products · Settlemint · Places · Capital · Design Systems. **A heading names what is actually inside it, in a word Curtis already uses.**
- **The three concentric circles are the grammar of one drawing, not a filing system.** No heading, label, blurb or tooltip in the Documents area borrows a word from them. Curtis: "whats order have to do with hardware. i think this is a silly organization tactic. nobody will get it" — and "i really don't even know what you mean about rings."
- **Places are locations.** Real ground Firma holds, builds on, or deploys into. Not the idea of place. A Realm is not a location: it is the personal edge someone carries between them. OPEN: "Districts of Heaven" sits on the shelf and names no ground — it is the Nation/Alliance/World structure paper, its twelve districts named for functions. Curtis's call whether it moves to The Canon.
- **Products, not hardware.** Realm, Haven and SeedBase, Compute, Photon. Firma Edge is the Sphere they sit inside, not a fifth product, so an Edge-wide document sits at the shelf root.
- **The Canon holds what governs; Theosis holds what forms.** Canon 0101, Vision 0101, the entity map and the Code of Conduct bind you. The prophetic and Orthodox papers form you. A prophetic paper is not a rule.
- **firmamint is the chain and every -mint layer on it**: Embodimint, Firmagentics and DNA, the atonemint economy, elemint, and the Commons. The Commons is a layer here and not hardware — it is Atonemint, Commons Netting, the elemints, the FIG reserve, Fragmints. The documents about the chain itself sit at the shelf root, because a shelf named firmamint cannot contain a folder also named firmamint.
- **THEOS is its own shelf**, the operating system of the Nation, flat. A folder that passes about twenty documents becomes a shelf.
- Domains stay a **filter**, with exactly two declared exceptions as shelves: **Theosis** and **Capital**. The funds sit in a Domain and in no system; the formation corpus describes no system. Everywhere else a Domain does filter work only.
- Ring, layer and Domain remain **filters over the whole library**, not folders: touching Labs lights the Firmaurica briefing and the Realm specs at once, wherever they are filed. **Domain is the one chip row on screen** — the 2026-09-05 ruling names Domains a filter. The circle and the layer stay facts in the data and `?ring=` / `?layer=` links that still narrow, with the words off the surface: a layer row drew the killed word "Hardware" as the only chip on the Products shelf, and drew "Place 19" beside a Places shelf of twelve.
- Firma Edge is a Sphere inside Labs, **not one of the fifteen systems**. An Edge-wide document belongs to no single product.
- Known gap, not a filing problem: **Treasury and rails is LIVE and has no document written about it.**
