# Never



**Money firewalls**
- FIRMA is never reserve-eligible; no FIG↔FIRMA corridor exists. (Terra, Fei)
- Buybacks spend only realized surplus above a full buffer. (Olympus)
- One asset, one claim: citizen vault and FIG backing are disjoint by address. (FTX rehypothecation)
- A premium on the system's own liability never marks up its own backing. (Olympus, Terra marks)
- Emission never outruns sinks. (Axie, Helium)
- No reputation bridge to any transferable asset. (veCRV / Convex)
- No discretionary gates anywhere. (Celsius, MMF 2020)
- FIRMA↔FIG stays a depth-capped minor venue. (Terra contagion)
- Reputation is decayable, revocable, never buyable; bonding curves price assets (FIRMA bonds, elemint offerings), never the trust axis.
- Flow enforcement never becomes lock-in: the chain stays permissionless, conversion routes stay open, direct ETH gas remains the escape hatch so the paymaster can never censor.
- FIG holders' money is never hostage to operator behavior; stewards and agents post FIRMA collateral for their mandate envelopes.
- FIRMA is earned via Sowing, never bought; FIG floats, not pegged.
- Idle capital is taxed toward seedbase, never rewarded; hoarding triggers demiurge of value and reputation loss.
- Loans repay by pool fee capture, never interest (anti-usury).
- Vault claims issue only against a VAULTED elemint, one claim per elemint (single-encumbrance registry). _(source: rulings · (late))_
- Money in the apps is cents, never floats; every payment goes through `PaymentRail`; nothing may reach around the doors (`ensure_embodimint`, `getTreasury`, `chain.ts`, `recordAct`).
- A job is PAID only on chain confirmation, never on submit; two clicks can never pay twice (atomic claim).
- Auto-verify moves work to READY_TO_PAY and never pays; the tithe is added to the poster's reservation, never deducted from the worker.
- The commons shares harvested rainwater, never sells it (Texas).
- The Firma Trust Annuity principal never deploys into atoms.
- Communities never pay; the Mint pays (deployment / protocol fee, never licensing).

**Identity and standing**
- Fragmints never transfer: inherit yes, sell no, pledge no.
- No reputation market, no bonding curve on standing.
- Capital never raises Fragmint capacity; only giving does.
- No manual entry where the chain already has the event ("a form is a place to lie; a Trace is not").
- No retroactive capacity; earned weight is settled, only new slices carry a new rate.
- Recall only on Canon-defined breach; speaking truth and leaving are never punishable (anti-Baal).
- Per human, never per wallet or container; splitting identity must never split reward. _(source: FM-ER §10 (FM-WM-001 D18))_
- Two ledgers (Fragmint slices = contribution; Embodimint = standing), never merged.
- Paid-at-FMV work earns no time-slices. _(source: PIE · §6.3 (proposed rule, unruled))_
- An elemint is a fingerprint signed to its host's Embodimint; it never moves and is never a token you hold. _(source: rulings · (late))_
- Reputation may only widen a bounded aperture that is non-zero at zero; endorsement counts distinct verifying counterparties, never engagement.
- Dimensions the book cannot witness read "no signal yet"; never guessed or padded.
- A face image is never on-chain (a hash at most).
- Settlemint is earned through T0→T4, never purchased.
- Nothing may branch on human-vs-agent in payment or permission code.
- Living at a Settlemint creates no Firma Role; a Role grants no authority over Settlemint life; Pod or Swarm participation joins no one to a Sphere; membership comes only from the Settlemint's own covenant.
- Sealed is a status of work, never of people; no team is the inner circle; compartmentalization by timing, never by trustworthiness.
- Individual sovereignty is never overridden; protection never collapses into control (Sovereignty Guard).
- Agent covenants burned at genesis (No Extraction and five more) cannot be revoked or amended.

**Governance principles**
- The Five Immutable Principles: Participation in the Divine by All Creation · Abundance Over Extraction (no cap tables; extraction architecturally impossible) · The Provision is for the Vision · Phased Disclosure (Phase 1 externally) · The Prophetic Correction Ratchet (baseline deeper, never shallower).
- Canon law is sealed and cannot be amended except by unanimous Vision Alliance vote.
- Build in protocol order; layers cannot be skipped or reordered; Core cannot be built without skilled engineers.
- EDEN never crosses into OpCo day-to-day management.
- Delegated authority is changed formally, never reclaimed informally; a brand is not automatically a Sphere; the map describes authority that exists, not ambition.
- Foundation Resolutions are sovereign acts, never "General Partner decisions".
- Constitutional documents are not binding law until ratified; the Order draft "supersedes when ratified".
- Public copy leads with neutral infrastructure; theology is not led with externally (Phase 1).
