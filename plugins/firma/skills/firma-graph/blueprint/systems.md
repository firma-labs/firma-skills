# The fifteen systems

| Ring | System | Status | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Center | **Embodimint** | LIVE | One person, one wallet, one record. The spindle every layer turns on. Rings: citizen of the Nation of Heaven · the alliance beside it · outside the Canon. |
| I · Protocol | **firmamint** | DESIGNED | Firma's own chain, the membrane to Base, and the rail money rides today. |
| I | **Firmagentics and DNA** | DESIGNED | The covenant every agent carries, and the protocol that catches drift. |
| I | **Atonemint economy** | DESIGNED | The protocol that makes the system whole after every trade. FIG lives here. |
| I | **Elemint** | DESIGNED | Digital fingerprints of real production, the vault, and the claims that trade. |
| I | **Treasury and rails** | LIVE | Where money sits and moves today: the community book, the rails, the earning vault. |
| I | **The Commons** | DESIGNED | Not hardware — one shared commons every deployment joins, beside Atonemint: Atonemint, Commons Netting, the elemints, the FIG reserve, Fragmints. Moved from the hardware ring 2026-09-11. |
| II · Order and hardware | **Firma Foundation** | SIGNED | Governs and protects the Order. Rings: public hat · quiet vehicles · sealed core. |
| II | **THEOS** | DESIGNED | The operating system of the Nation. You talk to theo; DNA governs it. Governed spine: Canon → DNA → THEOS → Firmagentics → theo. |
| II | **Realm** | DESIGNED | The personal edge: the node, the mesh, the phone, and the capture kits that attach. A sibling product family beside Edge, not a division of it (2026-09-11). |
| II | **Haven and SeedBase** | DESIGNED | A Haven is a dwelling. A SeedBase is a community Haven with every system a place needs. |
| II | **Compute** | DESIGNED | The hardware that earns: Sentinel, Mind, ARK, and Edge Cache. |
| II | **Photon** | DESIGNED | Panels, batteries, and the Convertor that turns sunlight into reserve. |
| III · The world | **Settlemint** | SIGNED | The stack a community earns stage by stage — observed cluster · governed settlement · reserve settlement · sovereign settlement · own chain — until it runs its own chain. T0–T4 labels retired 2026-09-11. |
| III | **Place and people** | SIGNED | Person, household, Realm, Settlemint, District, Nation. Campuses, colonies, and formation. |
