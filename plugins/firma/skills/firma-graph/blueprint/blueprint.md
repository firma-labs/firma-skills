# The Blueprint

**What it is.** One drawing of all Firma: 289 parts in 15 systems, 125 flows, the never rules, and the rulings, rendered as three concentric rings around [[embodimint]]. Ring I is the protocol (firmamint, Firmagentics and DNA, the Atonemint economy, Elemint, Treasury and rails). Ring II is the Order and its hardware (the Foundation, THEOS, Realm, Haven and SeedBase, Compute, Photon, the Commons). Ring III is the world (Settlemint, place and people). The five Domains are a filter that dims what is not theirs. Lines run; touching a system lights what it touches; opening a system reveals its parts in the same grammar. See [[systems]].

**What it must make clear** is in [[one-machine]]: one machine, people at the center, three rings always, abundance outward, honest status, it has to do something. Curtis: "we want to see how it all connects in a beautiful manner in concentric circles." It is not a story or replay mode.

**Where it lives.** Source `~/dev/firma-websites/sites/blueprint/` (Python build → one self-contained HTML; `dist/` is tracked). Preview at https://firma-blueprint.netlify.app; the hostname blueprint.firmalabs.org and the git link are Curtis's to do. The parts inventory of record is `firma-vault/engineering/builds/2026-09-05-firma-blueprint/10-MERGED-parts.md`; rulings land in `sites/blueprint/data/fixups.json` until the vault sheet is edited. The full export, `blueprint.md`, is rebuilt on every deploy and downloadable from the site; a snapshot sits beside this node as `blueprint-full.md`.

**Look.** Drawn in [[firma-edge-design]]: bone ground with a 24px drafting grid, ink, Field Orange for anything live or selected, Geist and Geist Mono, corner brackets, document plates. Phones first; verify at 390px.

**Words on screen.** "Systems", never "assemblies". Status words LIVE · SIGNED · DESIGNED · PROPOSED · SEALED. Every number a projection. The killed list applies; see [[killed-words]].

**Extending it.** Use the data as given. Never invent a part, a flow, or a number. A new ruling from Curtis goes into fixups, the vault sheet, and [[rulings]]. The lines Firma does not cross are in [[never]]. If a model or agent is asked to build or extend the Blueprint, hand it `blueprint-full.md` and this node.
