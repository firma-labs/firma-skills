# FIG

**"A currency truly for the people and by the people"** (Curtis, directed 2026-09-11). FIG **floats** against the network's productive output. It is not dollar-pegged. FIG is backed by a decentralized, distributed reserve: elemints (see [[elemint]]) and the real-world assets Elemint Exploration Mapping maps, plus agentic production and output — each unit signed to its producer's Embodimint, human or agent, Ambassadors included. Never "backed by" a government or its debt. Settlemint local coins (figmints) are **pegged to FIG**.

**Thermodynamic from the jump**, never retrofitted. Money has to be in liquidity pools or moving. The state is read as temperature (velocity), pressure (concentration of holdings), volume (liquidity depth), entropy (distribution), equilibrium (the composite target). Idle capital is progressively taxed toward seedbase (the Harberger idle tax). Hoarding triggers demiurge of value and reputation loss. "You gain far more by deploying than sitting." Loans repay by pool fee capture, never interest.

**The four instruments of the money layer:** FIG (transact) · elemints (store; see [[elemint]]) · FIRMA in two forms (sown, non-transferable → one-way transferable; earned by Sowing, never bought) · Reputation (stand, per human; see [[embodimint]]) plus a Stability Buffer. FIRMA is never reserve-eligible. The "60% cap" and "Cap Sentinel" are struck.

Mintware is the liquidity intelligence this runs on (Uniswap V4 singleton, one USDC pool, custom hooks). Nic Robinson owns that architecture; agents do not build the pool shape unilaterally. The money firewalls, learned from Terra, Olympus, FTX, Celsius, Axie, and veCRV, are in [[never]].

Every rate, cap, and yield is a projection. See [[numbers-are-projected]].
