# SeedBase

A SeedBase is the seed of life planted in a location: radical generosity and abundance you can stand inside. You experience it the same way at SeedBase HQ or at a SeedBase deployed to a community anywhere. From that seed grow the Realm mesh, the local economy, and in time a Settlemint.

Structurally, a SeedBase is a community [[edge-and-photon|Haven]] with every Edge and Realm system for self-sufficiency, Edge Cache included, sizes One · Two · Three · custom. A modular kit packaged with purpose, delivering structured water, compute, and energy by first need, added like blocks as a colony forms.

**Do not** frame SeedBase as "a deployment engine that converts capital into deployed sovereignty" or as restoration hardware, and do not split it from Firma Edge as a separate platform layer. The app that connects SeedBases, wallets, and giving is real but minor: "the technology serves the experience, not the other way around."

SeedBase is a Firma Edge division. Excess from every SeedBase flows outward; see [[earning-by-giving]].
