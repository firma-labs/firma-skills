# Earning by giving excess

Communities, households, and hosts do not sell their productive output. They give their excess to [[firmamint]]. The system mints e()-elemints in their name (see [[elemint]]). **The minting is the giving.** Reserve credits flow back to the host in proportion to the excess contributed. This is Abundance Over Extraction made operational.

**Anchor lines.** The subject matches the surface, and the preposition is canonical:
- **The power runs through firmamint.** Energy, Photon, deployment surfaces. Power flows.
- **The compute runs on firmamint.** Labs, chain, Compute, agentic surfaces. Compute executes.
Never "the compute runs through firmamint."

**Every product, deployment, partner, or marketing surface names three things:** firmamint (lowercase), the relevant e() elemint in its exact parenthesized form, and the earning-by-giving framing. If space allows only one, the order is elemint name, then firmamint, then the principle.

Wrong: "Sell your surplus solar back to the grid." Right: "Every kWh of excess is given to firmamint, which mints e(photon) in the community's name. The giving is the earning."

Policy over measurement on partner surfaces: "Communities never pay. Edge Cache pays." is policy and true. Any figure is a projection; see [[numbers-are-projected]].
