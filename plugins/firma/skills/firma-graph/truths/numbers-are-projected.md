# Every number is a projection

Curtis: "It's not measured yet. Nothing should be taken as exact figures."

The architecture, the protocol design, and the brand are current state. Every number is a forward projection: hardware output, revenue, recovery rates, liters per day, reward rates, timelines, reserve percentages. Vendor specs, engineering targets, and financial models do not qualify as measured, however reputable.

**In writing:** "The working model projects…", "The team's target is…", "Vendor-rated at…". Never "Edge Cache produces…" without the frame. Prefer policy over measurement on partner surfaces.

**A number becomes measured only when** a real Firma deployment ran for the period, the output was recorded on chain or in audited records, and the number cited is the actual measurement.

**Status words for parts and claims:** VERIFIED (measured) · LIVE (running today) · SIGNED (a charter, agreement, or relationship exists) · DESIGNED (specified, not built) · PROPOSED (an idea on the table) · SEALED (exists, deliberately undocumented). Sealed is a status of work, never of people.
