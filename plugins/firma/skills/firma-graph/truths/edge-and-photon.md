# Firma Edge and Firma Photon

```
            Firma Labs
           /          \
    Firma Edge      Firma Photon
    (hardware)      (energy)
    ├── Realm       ├── Photon (the panels; brand and product share the name)
    ├── SeedBase    ├── Ion+ (residential battery)
    ├── Haven       ├── Salt Ion+ (industrial sodium-ion)
    └── Compute     └── Atmos (atmospheric water capture array)
```

Photon partners with Edge on every deployment: Photon on the roof, Ion+ on the wall, Salt Ion+ on the pad, the **Photon Convertor** coordinating with Realm (see [[atonemint]]). Reserve accounting (kWh → e(photon) → FIG) crosses the boundary through firmamint.

**Compute** is the hardware that earns: Sentinel, Mind, ARK, and **Edge Cache**. Edge Cache is THE capital block: one Edge Compute product that earns whether it does storage, compute, inference, or mining ("cache and cash"). Outputs e(compute) and e(BTC). Its waste heat warms the dwelling. Lives in every SeedBase. "Communities never pay. Edge Cache pays." The word "Mint" is retired for the block; never write Edge Mint again.

**Haven** is a dwelling (home, office, or community space), sizes One · Two · Three · custom. **SeedBase** is a community Haven with every Edge and Realm system for self-sufficiency, Edge Cache included. Both are modular kits packaged with purpose, added like blocks as a place forms. The old sub-terms (Hub, cafe, pods, Lux) are gone. See [[seedbase]].

**Atmos** (locked 2026-09-06) replaces Spring: wall-mounted panels that pull water from the air into the tank, a capture kit on a Realm node sold beside Photon and Ion+. Water Realm is not a separate product: a Realm node plus a water capture kit. Realm is never divided by element. "Firma Pure" is personal branding, not canon.

**Orbital compute** is one PROPOSED part, "Orbital Edge". Pulsar, Nebula, and every prior orbital product name are pulled. Star Realm is connectivity like Starlink, under [[realm]].

Killed: Firma Energy, Edge Energy, Edge Base, SIP Grid Panel, modpod, Edge Mint. Every spec number is a projection. See [[killed-words]], [[numbers-are-projected]].
