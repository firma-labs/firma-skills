# Firma is the Order

**Firma is the name of the whole system, not one product.** The anchor sentence, the one the rest of the architecture hangs from:

> **Firma is a sovereign infrastructure ecosystem spanning software, assets, physical systems, and communities.**

Two ways to answer "what's Firma?" — the clean one and the human one:

> Firma is the sovereign infrastructure ecosystem behind the products, platforms, and deployments.

> Firma builds the systems that let people, places, and communities own more of the infrastructure they depend on.

## The functions inside it

| Name | Its job |
|---|---|
| **Firma Labs** | Builds the protocols, software, and core systems. |
| **Firma Frontier** | Builds and deploys physical infrastructure in the real world. |
| **Elemint** | The RWA platform. See [[elemint]]. |
| **firmamint** | The rights and settlement layer. See [[firmamint]]. |
| **Realm** | The human-facing infrastructure layer for homes, places, and communities. See [[realm]]. |

Lowercase **firmamint** holds even in this list. See [[names-and-casing]].

## The language this makes natural

- **"We are Firma."** We belong to the broader Firma ecosystem and mission. Cultural and institutional, not corporate — that is what makes the phrase strong.
- **"That's Firma."** That project, product, or behavior expresses the Firma way of building.
- **"Who built this?"** Answer with the function that did the work: *Firma Frontier deployed it. Elemint structured the asset. firmamint handles the rights. It serves a Realm.*
- **"What company is this?"** Depends on the context: *It's an Elemint product, part of Firma. It's a Firma Frontier deployment. It's built by Firma Labs.*

## The distinction to hold

**Firma = the Order. Frontier, Labs, Elemint, Realm, firmamint = distinct functions held inside it.**

Do not let "Firma" mean "the software company." When it does, everything collapses into Firma this, Firma that, Firma product, Firma platform, Firma deployment — and the names underneath stop meaning anything.

There is one sense of the word, not two. [[firma-order]] has said since 2026-08-11 that Firma is an Order in the pattern of the Order of Melchizedek — a covenant collective, not a company with an org chart — and this node says what that Order is called and how to speak about it. An earlier draft of this ruling called Firma "the house"; Curtis struck that metaphor on 2026-09-20 because it read as a holding company sitting above the work, which is the thing an Order is not.

What this does not change: the [[mission]] sentence stays verbatim, and "we build for the edges" still anchors Edge and its deployments rather than the whole. Nor does it change the sequencing: a person still meets a product first and finds the Order later.
