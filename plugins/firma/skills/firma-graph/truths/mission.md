# Mission

> We build sovereign infrastructure for the frontier and the reach — restoring agency, dignity, and prosperity to the people the current systems were not designed for.

Use it verbatim. Do not paraphrase a load-bearing sentence.

**The two edges.** The frontier is the builders working past where current systems were designed to operate. The reach is the communities past where current rails were built to reach. Both are first class. The hero lockup is `FIRMA LABS` / `[ we build for the edges ]`.

Every briefing, partner or team or public, opens with this before its own topic. See [[briefing-opening]]. What the mission creates can be stated as "a new operating system for humanity", carrying the full breadth of formation, governance, community, and economics, never reduced to software. See [[firma-order]].
