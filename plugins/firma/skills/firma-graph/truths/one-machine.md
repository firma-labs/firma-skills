# One machine

1. **It is one machine, not a list of products.** Every part exists because something flows through it: money, energy, water, compute, identity, authority. Show the flows, not just the boxes.
2. **People are the center.** [[embodimint]] (one person, one wallet, one record) is the spindle every layer turns on. Nothing sits above the person.
3. **Three concentric rings, always.** The visual grammar of the whole system, recurring at every scale like outer court, inner court, holy of holies. At the top level: the protocol core, the order and its hardware, the world. Never five rings, never a grid, never a tree. See [[blueprint]].
4. **Abundance over extraction.** Excess is given outward, never hoarded. Where a line carries surplus, draw it flowing from the person and the place to the commons. Hoarding has no line. See [[earning-by-giving]] and [[fig]].
5. **Honest status.** Every part is Live, Signed, Designed, Proposed, or Sealed. Every number is a projection. See [[numbers-are-projected]].
6. **It has to visibly do something.** Lines run. Touching a system lights what it touches. A static diagram with labels fails, however complete.

The five Domains (Foundation · Capital · Labs · Theosis · Frontier) are a filter laid over the machine, not a tree it hangs from. See [[firma-order]].
