# basemint

basemint is the membrane between the open agentic economy on Base (x402, USDC, agentic commerce) and sovereign settlement on [[firmamint]]. The word is **membrane**. "Bridge" is a killed word for it; see [[killed-words]].

The rails (x402, MPP, UCP, AP2) are commodities. Routing intelligence and sovereign settlement are the moats. Every agent making x402 USDC payments on Base is potential basemint traffic.

Today, Allotmint's USDC-on-Base rail (treasury, payouts, the /sow gift address, Base Pay) is the proto-basemint surface for colonies. Base stays the open-economy face; sovereign settlement moves to firmamint when it is live. See [[colony-intro-version]].
