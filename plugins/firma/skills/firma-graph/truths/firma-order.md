# The Firma Order

The Firma Order is the collective of all, in the pattern of the Order of Melchizedek. Instead of a company or an org chart, Firma *is* an Order: a covenant collective holding many fields of stewardship. Never present it as a company with a tagline. The Order framing gets one plain sentence in handbook docs; House of Saints governance is the spine.

**Five Domains, one verb each.** Foundation governs ✴ Capital stewards ✴ Labs builds ✴ Theosis forms ✴ Frontier establishes. The Domains are a filter over the machine, not a tree. Research sits inside Labs. Edge and Photon are Spheres inside Labs that may become entities. See [[one-machine]].

**Operating entities:** Firma Labs, Firma Edge, Firma Photon, Firma Research. Verbs: build, deploy, power, prove. "Governed by the House but legally separate. Each receives its mandate from above and operates freely within it." Ventures is not listed. Onym is the sealed part of the Capital team; describe it minimally.

**Governance shape:** Foundation governs. EDEN directs (the one Ministry inside the Foundation). The Trust holds. The operating entities execute. There are no "Ministries of X". CIK is ministry deployment under Theosis; Edge deploys, CIK accompanies. Foundation Resolutions are sovereign acts, never "General Partner decisions".

**Mottos.**
- Order: "The center holds shared purpose. The edges are trusted to act."
- Governance: "Authority moves down. Accountability moves up."
- Rally: "One project ✴ Five Spheres ✴ One table ✴ Every week."

"A new operating system for humanity" is one simple way to say what Firma creates. Humanity's operating system is formation of people, governance, purpose, prosperity, community, and economics, not just tech. See [[core-values]] and [[mission]].
