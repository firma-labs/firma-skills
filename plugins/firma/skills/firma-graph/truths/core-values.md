# Core Values, Ethos, Conduct

**The seven Core Values**, each written as an article (number · statement · anchor · body · prevents): Stewardship over Ownership · Abundance over Extraction · Honesty as Infrastructure · Meekness · Purpose over Objectives · Protect the Vulnerable · The Ratchet. Capstone lines, not values: "Build for the person, not the user" and "Love for neighbor."

**The Ethos is posture only** and never repeats the values: Radical faith in the impossible · Agentic-first · Provision precedes labor · Anti-Mammon, anti-Baal · Decisive peace · Name the fear.

**The Code of Conduct** (the name "The More Excellent Way" is retired for the title; the 1 Corinthians 13 content stays): the outer floor of six commitments (No extraction · No deception · No paternalism · Generosity as default · Transparency on the record · The mission governs) and the inner floor of the marks of how we treat one another. The fourteenth mark: "We do not gossip. It takes two to gossip."

**The Five Immutable Principles** sit above all of it: Participation in the Divine by All Creation · Abundance Over Extraction · The Provision is for the Vision · Phased Disclosure (neutral infrastructure externally, theology internal) · The Prophetic Correction Ratchet (baseline deeper, never shallower).

Theology is Orthodox covenant theology (theosis, kenotic, phronema), not "biblical principles". Calling is named plainly: Imago Dei, "not trying to be Christian, not trying not to be." Handbook documents get plain names ("Glossary", not "The Lexicon"). See [[formation]] and [[ethos-voice]].
