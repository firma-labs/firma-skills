# Embodimint

Embodimint is the person on chain: one person, one wallet, one record. In the [[blueprint]] it is the center spindle that every layer touches; people are never on an outer ring. Its three rings: citizen of the Nation of Heaven / the alliance beside it / outside the Canon.

**Six dimensions of standing** (the reputation vector): generosity ×3 · harmony ×2 · reliability ×1.5 · covenant compliance ×1.5 · community endorsement ×1 (distinct verifying counterparties, never engagement) · economic contribution ×1. Soulbound, non-transferable, per human never per wallet. Same schema for humans and agents. Jubilee: every seven epochs, returning to active generosity grants a boost; no one is permanently defined by their worst season. Dimensions the book cannot witness read "no signal yet", never guessed.

Do not confuse the six dimensions with the Phronema Engine's six pre-execution lenses. The composite formula has two versions in canon (six-term linear, five-term square-root); Allotmint runs the linear one pending Curtis's call. Never invent a third.

**Two faces of one identity:** **theo** talks to humans (chief of staff, friend, voice into THEOS). **The Ambassador** talks to agents (commerce, handshakes, proofs, Constraint Cards). Neither is "more public"; they face different audiences. See [[theos]].

Interim card: the 3D holo card at Allotmint /atx/me with a private face bucket; a face image is never on chain. Standing gets stronger bonding curves at firmamint; see [[colony-intro-version]]. Economic rights live beside standing in [[fragmint]].
