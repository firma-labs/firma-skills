# Settlemint and place

**Settlemint** is the stack a community earns through T0 → T4, never purchased. T3 Sovereign is the community's own sovereign treasury. The word SWF is reserved for the Nation's sealed fund and never appears as a rung. Figmint lets a Settlemint mint its own FIG-pegged coin; Governmint and Figmint are critical to the stack. Each Settlemint shards a portion of FIG reserve to stabilize its coin, and writes its own DNA-equivalent within Firmagentics.

**How a Settlemint begins.** A Settlemint begins when the community **claims it on-chain on firmamint**. It is claimed, not detected — a deliberate act by the people, never an automatic trigger fired by hardware.

**The criteria for that claim are not decided.** The team has not ruled them, and a hard rule written ahead of that decision breaks everything downstream. Direction only, not a threshold: some portion of the stack standing (firmamint shard, [[embodimint]], Allotmint, Governmint) and some density of Realm devices and capacity — storage, compute, energy, water. **Do not invent a device count, a percentage, or a checklist.** This ruling is owed by the team.

Killed with this ruling: "a Settlemint is not declared — it is detected", the "Realm Hub axiom", hardware-density auto-instantiation, and the "10+ devices" T0 trigger. Those were written into the wiki by an agent pass, never ruled, and they contradict earning the stack tier by tier. See [[killed-words]].

**The place ladder:** Person → Household → Realm → Settlemint → District → Network State. Realm is not a location on this ladder: it is the personal edge domain a person or household holds — devices, identity, agents, compute, energy, permissions, history — and carries between places. It belongs to the person or household, never to the Settlemint. The Nation of Heaven is a Network State, the one Firma is building. Constellation sits beside the ladder. Composition, not containment. There is no "Colony" rung.

Living at a Settlemint creates no Firma Role, and a Role grants no authority over Settlemint life; membership comes only from the Settlemint's own covenant. Allotmint and the Water Realm ledger are Settlemint's financial layer (community treasuries), not Frontier's.

The Colony apps (Allotmint, Sojourn, Hive Mind, Increase, 1Accord, Converge) are the living-lab surfaces of the ATX Settlemint today. See [[colony-intro-version]] and [[colony-design-law]].
