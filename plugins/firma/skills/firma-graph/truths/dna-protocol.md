# DNA Protocol

| Word | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Trace** | One thing happened, written down. Atomic, immutable, queryable. |
| **Pulse** | The rhythm of one entity, aggregated from its traces. |
| **Heartbeat** | The rhythm of the whole organism. All pulses together. |
| **DNA Protocol** | The conductor that listens to the heartbeat, catches drift, routes signals, calls the Council when needed. |

DNA is Divine Nation Alliance, the governing body. DNA Protocol is two things at once: the coded rule set inside every Firma agent (autonomic; not a service, not a sidecar) and the Council's module inside [[theos]] (deliberative, Council only). Neither alone is DNA Protocol.

**Firmagentics vs DNA.** Firmagentics is the species genome: the eight-layer moral protocol shared by everyone on [[firmamint]], burned at genesis, never overridable. DNA Protocol is the organism genome: Firma's own operational governance, ratifiable within Firmagentics. Each Settlemint writes its own DNA equivalent.

The coded layer's cycle: Monitor → Route → Surface → Correct. Restoration first: Redirect → Restrict → Sandbox → Suspend → Retire. Authority tiers: agent → chief of staff → Minister → Council → Vision Stewards → Vision Alliance. Privacy is pierced by impact graphs, never content.

**Reject:** "DNA Heartbeat" as one concept, "DNA agent", Pulse as a synonym for Trace or metric, Heartbeat as a synonym for Pulse, DNA as an API. The canon monitor that catches drift in documents is DNA's job once live; until then, review by hand.
