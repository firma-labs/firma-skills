# Formation

**Purpose, locked:** God pulls people from all walks of life, many with no corporate, agentic, or tooling experience. We assess to learn where they are so we can be deliberately developmental. Never present the week or the rubric as a screening gate.

**The instrument:** twelve traits in four groups, each written as statement · anchor · prevents. Character (Integrity, Accountability, Transparency, Servanthood) · Drive (High agency, Self-starter, Brings solutions, Dreamer and Doer) · Inner life (Self-awareness, Emotional regulation, High faith and speaks life, weighted most heavily) · Mind (Horsepower and judgment). Scale 1–4, no rating without evidence. Sequence: self-assessment → the three assessments (DISC, MBTI, working styles) → rater pass → gap review (self versus observed is the headline). Baseline, not verdict; re-score at 30 and 90 days. Traits sit beside the seven [[core-values]]: values gate the door, traits shape the profile.

**Servant Hearted Onboarding** is a one-week shared trial: arrive low → serve → the three assessments → know your people and join the Colony → the table → the press and grit day → the review. Everyone holds one unglamorous shared commitment, founder included, visible, weekly (The Deep End). Earning follows serving. Motto: "We go low and let the Lord elevate us." Forms honor, gratitude, unity.

**Oikos** is the team's workstyle card (reach, hours, focus, feedback, decisions, helps me, please don't) at portal.firmalabs.org/oikos; publishing one is standard by day three.

Documents are clean native Google Docs numbered 01–04 in his scheme, jargon-free. See [[team-docs-plain]].
